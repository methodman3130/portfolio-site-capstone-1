01 / About

Technology with purpose.

Hello! I’m Vitruvius John D. Barayuga, originally from Ilocos Sur and currently based in Baguio City, Philippines.

I am an Associate Professor at the University of the Cordilleras and serve as Director for Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies under the Office of the Vice President for Research, Innovation, and Institutional Planning.

My work brings together higher education, responsible innovation, applied research, and emerging technology. I am especially interested in turning new technologies into practical initiatives that create value for institutions and communities.
